About the Artist

Colour, patience & a little bit of soul.

Hi, I'm Hetvi. My studio is a place for patient looking — small gestures, close attention, nothing added without reason. From delicate graphite portrait sketches to bold acrylic textures and devotional god & goddess art, every piece begins with a story and stays with the material until it says something true.
