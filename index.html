About the artist

The artist behind the canvas

Hi, I'm Hetvi — an artist who finds magic in turning faces, feelings and blank walls into something worth remembering. My hands move between graphite, acrylic, watercolour and texture, but the goal stays the same: art that feels personal.

Whether it's a quiet portrait of someone you love, a goddess painted in gold, or a textured piece made just for your living room — every commission is built around your story, not a template.
